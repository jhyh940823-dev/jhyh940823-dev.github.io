CheeseMemo Support

A simple place for notes, with a little personality.

Where are my notes stored?

Your notes stay on your device. CheeseMemo has no app account or developer-operated sync server. Export a backup file in Settings before uninstalling the app or changing devices. Automatic backups inside the app can be lost with the device; keep a separate exported copy.

Moving to another device

Export a CheeseMemo backup on your old device and import that file on your new device. Restoring a subscription does not restore notes.

Ads appear despite my subscription

Sign in to the store with the Apple or Google account used for purchase, connect to the internet, and choose Restore purchases in app Settings. Purchases do not transfer between iOS and Android. Pending payments must be approved by the store before benefits apply.

Cancellation and refunds

Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. See the subscription guide for management and refund links.

Contact support

Include your app version, device, operating system, and steps that led to the problem. Do not send private note contents, passwords, or payment card numbers. Support correspondence is kept for one year after your inquiry is closed, except where a different legal retention period applies.
