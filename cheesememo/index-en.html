CheeseMemo Support

A simple place for notes, with a little personality.

Where are my notes stored?

Your notes stay on your device. CheeseMemo has no app account or developer-operated sync server. Export a backup file in Settings before uninstalling the app or changing devices. Automatic backups inside the app can be lost with the device; keep a separate exported copy.

Finding words in photos

Once recognition finishes, search for Korean or English text in attached photos and scans. Opening a match highlights it in the image. Recognition may take a moment; blurry text and handwriting can be inaccurate. See Privacy for SDK processing details.

Moving to another device

Starting with CheeseMemo 1.2.0 for Apple devices, enable Settings → iCloud Sync on each device signed into the same Apple Account. Notes, folders, photos, scans, themes, and fonts are stored in your private iCloud database. The app backs up local notes before connecting and merges both libraries. Concurrent note edits are kept in conflict copies. Changes also sync in the background when Apple allows it. Delivery can be delayed by network, battery, or background refresh settings; after force-quitting, reopen the app. Remote edits to a note you are writing merge after you close that editor. Check progress in iCloud Sync settings.

For older versions or Android, export a CheeseMemo backup on your old device and import it on your new device. Restoring a subscription does not restore notes. Sync does not replace a separate backup: deletions also sync to other devices.

Ads appear despite my subscription

Sign in to the store with the Apple or Google account used for purchase, connect to the internet, and choose Restore purchases in app Settings. Purchases do not transfer between iOS and Android. Pending payments must be approved by the store before benefits apply.

Marked text and previous versions

Open Marked text in the folder menu to revisit marked text in titles and bodies and share cards. Previous versions, in the editor’s More menu, restore as new notes without replacing the original. Version history is not included in regular backups; restore versions you want to keep as notes first. See the Plus guide for limits and access after expiry.

Cancellation and refunds

Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. See the subscription guide for management and refund links.

Contact support

Include your app version, device, operating system, and steps that led to the problem. Do not send private note contents, passwords, or payment card numbers. Support correspondence is kept for one year after your inquiry is closed, except where a different legal retention period applies.
