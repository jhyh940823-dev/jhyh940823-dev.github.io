Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026 · TORY Studio (박정현)

This policy covers CheeseMemo and its support pages. The app is preparing for store launch; ads and subscriptions are planned for launch. We do not operate a note-storage or sync server or an app account system. Advertising, payments, email support, and this website use the external services described below.

Records on your device

Notes, folders, photos, handwriting, stickers, settings, and in-app backups are stored on your device. We do not automatically collect them or include them in ad requests. Photos are imported when you select them. Your operating system handles biometric data; the app receives only the authentication result.

When you export, back up, or share a file, a copy goes to the app or location you select. External copies and operating system backups are subject to your settings and the relevant service's policy.

Subscriptions and payments

Apple App Store or Google Play processes payments and refunds. The app checks product identifiers, signed transactions or purchase tokens, and subscription status on your device. We do not send receipts or tokens to a developer-operated server or receive payment card numbers. Stores retain their own records under their policies and legal obligations.

Ads and privacy choices

The free note list uses Google AdMob and its User Messaging Platform (UMP). Ad personalization is disabled in the app. Android's advertising ID permission is removed, and iOS does not request tracking permission. We do not include a separate note analytics SDK.

Google's advertising SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app and ad interactions, diagnostic and performance data, and app- or device-scoped identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and diagnostics. Disabling personalization does not eliminate all data processing. Note titles, contents, folder names, photos, and search terms are not sent in ad requests.

Where required, regional privacy choices are presented. When available, use Advertising privacy choices in app Settings to change or withdraw consent or opt out of covered processing. The app does not request ads while consent or subscription status cannot be established. Changing privacy choices does not delete your notes.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google partner services information for data, purposes, and retention. Google's own retention depends on processing purposes, settings, security, and legal needs; we do not control its retention periods.

Support inquiries and retention

If you email us, we use your email address, display name, message and attachments, and app or device information you provide to respond, resolve problems, and handle disputes. Do not send sensitive note contents, passwords, or payment card numbers.

We keep support correspondence for one year after the inquiry is closed, then delete it. Records subject to a different legal retention requirement are kept only for that required period. You may request earlier deletion; we will explain any obligation that prevents it. Electronic records are deleted using available permanent-deletion controls and unnecessary paper copies are shredded.

Service providers and international processing

No developer-operated note server does not mean no external advertising, payment, email, or hosting servers.

  • Google LLC and affiliates: Advertising information is transmitted when ads are requested. Support correspondence is received and stored through Gmail. Processing may occur in the United States and other countries where Google operates facilities. Our support-copy retention is one year; Google's own records follow its policy. Transfer safeguards · Facility locations · Privacy contact.
  • Apple / Google stores: The platforms process account and transaction information for purchases and restoration. We do not receive your payment credentials. Apple policy · Google policy.
  • GitHub, Inc.: Visiting these support pages may involve processing IP addresses and access information for hosting and security in the United States and other service locations. These pages contain no separate advertising or visitor analytics scripts. See GitHub's policy for retention and international processing.

Where required, international processing relies on consent or another applicable legal basis. Refusal or withdrawal may limit some advertising, support, or online payment features; local notes remain. Contact us below to request restriction or deletion of support records we hold.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, a copy, or portability. We ask only for information needed to verify a request and respond within applicable deadlines. If we must refuse, we explain why and how to appeal. We cannot access records stored only on your device; use the app to view, export, or delete them.

In the EEA and UK, relevant bases include performance of a contract for requested features, legitimate interests in support and security, consent where required for advertising, and legal obligations. You may complain to your local supervisory authority. Applicable US state opt-out rights for sale, sharing, or targeted advertising can be exercised through available privacy choices and our contact address. Exercising rights does not restrict basic note-taking.

Teens and children

CheeseMemo is a general note app for teens and adults, not designed or marketed for children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Where local law requires parental consent, use the app with your parent or guardian. If we learn that a child's personal information has reached us, for example through support, we take appropriate steps such as deletion. Do not send birth dates or identity documents.

Deletion, security, and updates

Manage local records through note, trash, and backup controls. Delete exported copies separately in their destination. Uninstalling may remove local records. Deleting notes or uninstalling does not cancel a subscription.

Service connections use encrypted transport and support access is limited to responsible personnel. We update this page and its effective date when the policy changes, and provide any additional notice required for material changes.
