Monthly or yearly

Choose a one-month or one-year subscription. Planned prices in South Korea are KRW 990 per month or KRW 9,900 per year. Other regions use local store prices. Your purchase screen and the store confirmation show the actual price, currency, and billing period. The yearly subscription is charged once per year.

Renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled through your store account. Cancel before the renewal deadline shown by your store; Apple advises cancellation at least 24 hours before renewal. After cancellation, benefits normally remain until the paid period ends. Your existing notes and already-placed stickers remain editable after your subscription ends. Adding new premium stickers and removing ads are subscription benefits.

Purchases, restoration, and checking current benefits may require an internet connection. When Android restarts offline, adding new premium stickers waits until the purchase can be checked.
