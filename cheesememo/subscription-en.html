CheeseMemo Plus

Make your notes your own with every theme and sticker, and write without list ads.

Included with Plus

  • Every theme: coordinated notes, lists, folders and buttons
  • Every sticker: Basic, Crayon, Pixel, Chalk, Glow, Cheese and Little Garden
  • Marked text: collect underlined text and text-linked pen marks from titles and bodies, jump to their source, share themed cards, or combine them into a new note
  • Automatic collections: unfinished tasks, photos, scans and links, with saved search conditions
  • Previous versions: restore retained content as a new note while keeping the original
  • Rediscover: revisit older records
  • Little Garden: a coordinated theme, folders, buttons, 12 stickers and 3 templates
  • Removal of list ads

Basic writing, folders, search, handwriting, photos, tables, scans and file backup remain free. The Basic and Cheese themes and 12 Basic stickers are also free.

Previous-version limits

Versions are stored on your device at editing-session boundaries, up to the latest 20 per note and 200 MB overall; the oldest versions are removed first. This is not a record of every keystroke. Regular backup exports do not include version history, and deleting the app removes it. Restore a version as a new note before including it in a file backup.

Monthly or yearly

Choose a one-month or one-year subscription. On the South Korean App Store, Plus costs ₩990 per month or ₩9,900 per year. Other regions use local store prices. Your purchase screen and the store confirmation show the actual price, currency, and billing period. The yearly subscription is charged once per year.

Renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled through your store account. Cancel before the renewal deadline shown by your store; Apple advises cancellation at least 24 hours before renewal. After cancellation, benefits normally remain until the paid period ends. Your existing notes and already-placed stickers remain editable after your subscription ends. Applied designs and saved collection conditions are retained, and remaining previous versions can still be read and restored as copies. The Marked text, automatic collections, rediscovery, collecting new versions, applying new premium themes, stickers and Little Garden templates and removing ads require an active subscription.

Restore purchases and offline use

Restore purchases using the same store account on the same platform. iOS and Android purchases are not shared automatically. Purchases, restoration, and checking current benefits may require an internet connection. When Android restarts offline, features requiring an active subscription wait until the purchase can be checked. Existing notes and stickers remain editable.
