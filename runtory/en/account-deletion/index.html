Delete your account in the app

Signed-in users can request account deletion from RUNTORY Settings → Delete account. Deletion begins after the user reviews and acknowledges the notice.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase Auth account and normal server-side personal/related data
  • Account backups (Firestore/Storage) and uploaded running routes/photos
  • Friend codes, relations, and related reference data
  • Running records, routes, photos, pet/challenge data stored on this device
  • Your location collection/use fact records and queued location-fact uploads where no separate legal retention basis applies
  • Short-lived account-bound subscription caches, owner bindings, and similar operational data

What is not cancelled or is retained separately

  • App Store subscription: account deletion does not cancel it. Charges may continue; manage it separately in the App Store.
  • Device photo-library originals: RUNTORY does not delete originals stored in the Photos library.
  • OS location permission: change this separately in iPhone Settings.
  • Minimal subscription transaction/verification ledger: for legally required commerce records and settlement/dispute handling, minimized data such as hashed purchase/user identifiers, product/plan, verification status/time, expiration/cancellation/refund timing, and minimal correlation information is retained for 5 years from the last verification/processing date, then destroyed. Payment-card and bank-account details are not included.

Legally retained records are segregated from ordinary service data with restricted access. If operationally necessary, including after service termination, required records may be moved from Firebase to encrypted separate storage and are destroyed when the remaining legal retention period ends.

Processing time

Deletion runs in multiple steps including reauthentication, server cleanup, authentication-account deletion, and local cleanup. Success is not shown until the required steps complete; temporary failures can be retried in the app.
