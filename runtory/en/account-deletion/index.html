Account Deletion

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Delete in the app

Signed-in users can request deletion in Settings → Delete Account. In the current CloudKit configuration, required remote cleanup must complete before local-only teardown is reported as successful.

RUNTORY data deleted

  • CloudKit private RUNTORY identity data
  • the user's CloudKit account-backup zone, including structured backup, route and app-managed photo assets
  • current-account friend-code/relationship data
  • user-scoped local Drift data, routes, app-managed photos and game progress
  • the current user's location-use fact records through the applicable deletion flow

Items managed separately

  • App Store subscription: not automatically cancelled; cancel separately in Apple subscription settings.
  • Apple payment/transaction records: managed by Apple under its policies and legal obligations.
  • Photos kept in the user's iPhone photo library are not deleted by RUNTORY account deletion.

Sign in with Apple access

After RUNTORY data deletion, the Apple-account connection can remain in Apple's Sign in with Apple settings. The user can manually remove RUNTORY access from the Apple account settings.
