Delete your account in the app

Signed-in users can request account deletion from RUNTORY Settings → Delete account on a dedicated guidance screen. Deletion starts only after you review and acknowledge the notice.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase Auth account and personal data stored on our servers
  • Account backups (Firestore/Storage) and uploaded running routes or photos
  • Friend codes, relations, and related reference data
  • Running records, routes, photos, and pet/challenge data stored on this device

What is not cancelled or removed automatically

  • App Store subscription — not cancelled by account deletion; charges may continue. Manage it in the App Store first.
  • Device photo library originals — the app does not delete originals in your Photos library.
  • OS location permission — change it in the iPhone Settings app.
  • Subscription verification / anti-abuse transaction records — limited records may be retained for a period for security and settlement.

Processing time

Deletion runs in multiple steps (reauthentication, server cleanup, auth account delete, local cleanup). Success is shown only after those steps finish. If a step fails, you can retry in the app.
