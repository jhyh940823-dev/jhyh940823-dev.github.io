Location-based Service Terms

Last updated: 2026-08-05

Privacy Policy Terms of Use Location-based Service Terms Support Account Deletion Subscription

1. Service

During an outdoor run explicitly started by the user, RUNTORY uses GPS to calculate route, distance, time, and pace. Location may continue within OS-permitted limits while the screen is locked or another app is open. Indoor running can be used without location.

2. Storage, backup, and restore

Raw GPS route geometry may be retained on the device for up to 365 days. Coordinates from completed runs may be backed up and restored as compressed assets in Firebase Storage under the signed-in Firebase account. Route assets may include point order, latitude, longitude, and UTC timestamps.

Firestore may store a restore manifest containing the storage path, hash, size, format, and relationship identifiers. Public download URLs and local absolute device paths are not stored in the remote manifest. Generated overlays, result images, map snapshots, and exported sharing files are not currently included in route backup.

Route data is processed to provide user-requested account backup and restore. It is not sold or provided for advertising purposes.

3. Deletion and withdrawal

Users can delete individual records or withdraw location consent in Settings. Withdrawal deletes locally stored raw route geometry and prevents new outdoor GPS runs. Withdrawal may be restricted during an active run for record integrity and safety and can be completed after the run ends.

Account deletion requests cleanup of the Firebase Auth account, remote profile, Friends data, structured backup, and account-scoped route assets in Firebase Storage. A safe retry state may remain during temporary network or server failures. Distance, duration, pace, and other summaries may remain until separately deleted.

4. External processing

Firebase Storage and Firestore are used for account-scoped route backup and restore. Processing by Apple, Firebase, and other service providers follows the Privacy Policy and the providers' own policies. RUNTORY does not use precise route data for medical, emergency rescue, navigation, or advertising targeting purposes.

RUNTORY is available only to users aged 14 and over. GPS and sensor results may be inaccurate, delayed, or lost depending on the device, network, and surroundings. RUNTORY is not a medical, emergency rescue, or navigation service.
