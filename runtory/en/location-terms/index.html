Article 1 (Purpose and operator)

These terms govern RUNTORY's outdoor GPS running records and account route backup/restore. Provider: 멍스토어; representative: 박정현; address: 서울시 금천구 두산로3길 17; contact: support.runpet@gmail.com.

Article 2 (Service)

When the user starts an outdoor run, RUNTORY uses device location to record route, distance, duration and pace. Location may continue while the active run is backgrounded where iOS permits. Indoor running does not require a GPS route. Signed-in iOS users can use CloudKit account backup/restore that may include completed-run routes.

Article 3 (Collection and use)

Precise location, coordinate order/timestamps and derived running metrics are used for the running record, record viewing and account backup/restore requested by the user. RUNTORY does not currently use precise GPS routes for friend ranking or advertising.

Article 4 (Retention and deletion)

Local raw route geometry for a completed run is removed on the next normal app launch after the run becomes older than 365 days. Non-location summaries may remain. CloudKit backup has a separate lifecycle and may contain precise route assets; the current account-deletion path deletes the user's backup zone. Personal location information is to be destroyed when no longer needed for the service, subject to applicable law.

Article 5 (Location use-fact records)

RUNTORY may create minimum records needed for Korean location-information compliance and user rights. These records do not contain raw latitude/longitude; they can contain hashed subject/event identifiers, timestamps, use method and whether third-party provision occurred. The current record stores a six-month retention deadline. Withdrawal/account deletion triggers deletion of the current user's corresponding records.

Article 6 (User rights)

Users may withdraw all or part of location consent and request temporary suspension. They may request access/notice of their own location collection/use/provision fact records and the reason/content of any legally required third-party provision, and may request correction of errors. Use the in-app controls, iOS permission settings or support.runpet@gmail.com.

Article 7 (Third-party provision and processors)

RUNTORY does not currently provide a persistent precise-location sharing service to user-designated third parties. CloudKit processes route assets for the user's account backup/restore. The current Friends and advertising boundaries do not receive precise GPS routes. A future third-party location-sharing feature would require the applicable notice, consent and notification process before launch.

Article 8 (Users under 14 and safeguards)

RUNTORY is limited to users aged 14 or older and does not provide a guardian-consent path for under-14 location processing. Safeguards include minimum handling scope, access control, owner verification, release-log restrictions for precise routes, use-fact records and deletion/withdrawal procedures.
