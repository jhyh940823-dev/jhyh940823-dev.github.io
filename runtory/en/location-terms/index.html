1. Purpose

These Terms describe the conditions for RUNTORY's outdoor GPS running, route backup/restore, and related location-based services provided by 멍스토어, together with the rights of users whose personal location information is processed.

2. Service

When a user starts an outdoor run and GPS preparation begins, RUNTORY uses location information to calculate route, distance, time, and pace. Location may continue within OS-permitted limits while the screen is locked or another app is open. Indoor running can be used without location.

3. Personal location storage and backup

Raw GPS route geometry may be retained on the device for up to 365 days. Coordinates from completed runs may be backed up and restored as account-scoped compressed assets in Firebase Storage and may include point order, latitude, longitude, and UTC timestamps.

Firestore may store a restore manifest containing storage path, hash, size, format, and relationship identifiers. Route information is processed to provide running records and user-requested account backup/restore and is not used for advertising targeting or sold to third parties.

Personal location information is destroyed in a manner intended to prevent practical restoration when the service purpose is achieved or the user deletes/withdraws it, except where a separate legal retention basis applies.

4. Location collection/use fact records

RUNTORY automatically records and preserves confirmation records for collection and use of location information as required by Korean location-information law. These fact records include items such as the requester, collection/use time, collection method, acquisition path, and use method and do not include the actual latitude/longitude coordinates.

RUNTORY's retention period for these fact records is 6 months from the record date. They are automatically destroyed after the period. If the user withdraws location consent or deletes the account, the user's fact records without a separate legal retention basis are destroyed without delay even before six months.

Fact records are currently managed in secure server storage. If operationally necessary, including service termination, required records may be moved to encrypted separate storage. The same retention period, access controls, and destruction obligations continue to apply.

5. Location rights and how to exercise them

Users may withdraw all or part of their location collection/use consent at any time, request temporary suspension of collection/use, request access or notice regarding their own location collection/use/provision fact records, and request correction of errors.

These rights may be exercised through RUNTORY Settings → Data Management, by email at support.runpet@gmail.com, or by phone at 010-2682-8230. RUNTORY may request the minimum verification needed to confirm the requester.

6. Deletion and withdrawal

Users can delete individual running records or withdraw location consent in Settings. Withdrawal deletes related raw route geometry on the device and the user's server-side location-use fact records where no separate legal retention basis exists, and prevents new outdoor GPS runs. During an active run, withdrawal may be completed after the run ends to preserve record integrity.

Account deletion requests cleanup of the Firebase Auth account, remote profile/Friends/structured backup data, account-scoped route/photo assets, and the account's location-use fact records. A safe retry state may remain until temporary network/server errors are resolved.

7. External processing and third-party provision

Firebase Storage and Firestore support account-scoped route backup/restore and location-use fact management, and Firebase Cloud Functions support fact recording/deletion and retention cleanup. See the Privacy Policy for processor and cross-border processing details.

RUNTORY does not currently operate a location-based feature that provides personal location information to third parties, and does not use precise route information for advertising targeting or sale. If such a feature is introduced later, the legally required consent/notice process will be applied first.
