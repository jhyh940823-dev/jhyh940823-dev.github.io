1. Purpose

These Terms govern the RUNTORY mobile app provided by 멍스토어 and the rights and obligations of users and the operator. RUNTORY provides running records, pet growth, Friends, account backup and restore, iOS subscriptions, and ads for free users.

2. Service

RUNTORY provides outdoor and indoor running, pet and egg growth, quests, challenges, badges, collection, inventory, Firebase Friends, account backup and restore, RUNTORY Plus, and ad-supported free benefits.

GPS, motion, pace, distance, calories, route and voice guidance are convenience features, not medical, emergency, rescue, navigation, or fitness-safety services. Results may be inaccurate, delayed, unavailable or lost depending on the device, sensors, network and environment. Outdoor running requires location-based-service consent and OS permission.

3. Eligibility and account

RUNTORY is intended for users aged 14 and over. The current commercial iOS release requires Firebase Auth and Sign in with Apple. Users must complete required consent and age-14+ confirmation after authentication before entering the main app.

Users must protect their sign-in method and device access and must not misuse another person’s account, friend code, or data.

4. User obligations

Users must not interfere with service operation or security, use unauthorized automation, falsify transactions, abuse payments or ad rewards, manipulate rankings, violate others’ rights, or reverse engineer where prohibited by law.

5. Content and data

Primary data may be stored in the on-device database and the authenticated account’s Firebase services. Supported game, settings and summary data is stored in Firestore. Completed-run route points and app-managed original record photos may be backed up and restored through owner-scoped Firebase Storage.

Generated overlays/result images, map snapshots and share-export files are not included in the current backup classes. Signing out does not delete all local data. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete remote account data, backup assets or an App Store subscription.

6. RUNTORY Plus and billing

RUNTORY Plus is offered on the iOS App Store as monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions. The App Store displays the actual price, currency and billing date. Purchase and restore results are applied to the signed-in RUNTORY account after Apple transaction processing and Firebase server verification.

Banner and rewarded ads are removed for a server-verified Plus entitlement. To prevent renewal, cancel within Apple’s required time in Apple subscription settings. Billing methods, charges and refunds follow Apple policies. Deleting the RUNTORY account or uninstalling the app does not cancel the App Store subscription. Android billing and production ads remain on hold.

7. Friends and ranking

Friend codes, requests, relationships, lists and activity summaries are linked to the authenticated Firebase account. Friend ranking is motivational reference information, not an official competition record or basis for prizes. Sensor error, sync delay or network conditions may affect displayed results.

8. Advertising and service changes

Google Mobile Ads banners and rewarded ads may be shown to free users. Ads are requested only when the consent system permits requests. The launch configuration uses restricted non-personalized requests and does not request ATT. RUNTORY Plus users do not receive ad requests.

Advertising-provider processing and privacy choices follow the Privacy Policy and the in-app advertising privacy-options surface. Personalized ads, ATT, partner, mediation, or processing-scope changes will be activated only after required terms, privacy notices, consent, and store disclosures are updated.

9. Account deletion and limitation of liability

Users may request cleanup of the Firebase Auth account, remote profile, Friends, backup, owner-scoped Storage assets and local owner data through the in-app account deletion flow. A temporary server failure may leave the deletion flow in a safe retry state.

Nothing excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded. Liability related to device, network, permission, sensor, Apple, Firebase, Google Mobile Ads, or map-provider failures may be limited to the extent permitted by law.
