Article 2 (Service)

RUNTORY provides outdoor/indoor running records, pet and challenge gameplay, friends, CloudKit-based iOS account backup/restore, RUNTORY Plus and ads for free users. GPS, motion, pace, distance, calorie estimates and voice guidance are convenience features, not medical, emergency, navigation or safety-guarantee services.

Article 3 (Eligibility and account)

RUNTORY is intended for users aged 14 or older. The current iOS account path uses Sign in with Apple and CloudKit identity. The required age confirmation and legal consents must be completed before main service access.

Article 4 (User obligations)

Users must not misuse another person's account or data, interfere with service security, falsify purchases, manipulate ad rewards or rankings, or use prohibited automation or unlawful means.

Article 5 (Content and data)

Core data may be stored locally. Signed-in iOS backup may also contain structured data, precise completed-run routes and app-managed original record-photo copies in CloudKit. The current Friends adapter handles minimal public profile/friend-code/relationship/weekly aggregate data and excludes GPS routes, photos, email and authentication tokens.

Logging out does not delete all local data.

Article 6 (RUNTORY Plus and billing)

RUNTORY Plus is offered as monthly and annual auto-renewable subscriptions through the iOS App Store. Product name, term, actual price/currency and billing conditions are those shown in the purchase interface and App Store. Current benefits are removal of banner/rewarded ads, running-record photo attachment, two additional daily food items and one additional R currency on weekly-quest completion.

Purchase, renewal, payment method, cancellation and refund are handled through Apple's App Store payment system and applicable rules. Purchase restore is available in the app. Deleting the RUNTORY account or app does not cancel the App Store subscription. Nothing in these terms limits non-waivable consumer rights.

Article 7 (Advertising and friends)

Free users may receive Google Mobile Ads. The first iOS release uses Limited Ads, NPA/RDP and disables publisher first-party ID without requesting ATT. App-side ad requests are blocked for Plus users. Friend rankings are motivational reference information, not official competition results.

Signed-in users can request account deletion in Settings. The CloudKit path cleans remote friend data and private account/backup data before deleting user-scoped local data. Temporary network/service errors can leave a safe retry state rather than falsely reporting completion.
