RUNTORY Plus

RUNTORY Plus is offered on the iOS App Store as monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions. The App Store displays the actual price and currency for your storefront.

Benefits

  1. Remove all ads
  2. Add photos to running records
  3. Automatically receive 2 extra food items each day
  4. Receive 1 extra R currency with the weekly quest reward

Free users may see Google Mobile Ads banners and rewarded ads. For a server-verified RUNTORY Plus entitlement in active or grace status, banner space is removed and rewarded-ad requests are blocked.

Food policy

Daily food is capped at 3, and unused food does not carry over. The subscription provides an additional grant but does not increase the daily cap.

Purchase and restore

Signed-in users can choose a product and complete payment through the App Store. Purchase and restore results are applied to the corresponding RUNTORY account only after Apple transaction processing and Firebase server verification.

RUNTORY does not grant benefits while catalog loading or verification is incomplete. The app may show a preparing or retry state until store and verification services respond.

Renewal, cancellation, and refunds

The subscription is linked to your Apple ID and renews automatically. To avoid renewal, cancel in Apple subscription settings at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Billing methods, cancellation, and refunds follow Apple policies.

Deleting your RUNTORY account or uninstalling the app does not cancel an App Store subscription. Manage or cancel it separately in Apple subscription settings.

Platform scope

The current commercial subscription and advertising rollout is iOS-first. Android in-app billing and production ads remain on hold.

Customer support
