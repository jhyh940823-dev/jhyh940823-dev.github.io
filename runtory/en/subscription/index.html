Products

RUNTORY Plus is offered through the iOS App Store as monthly and annual auto-renewable subscriptions. The purchase interface and App Store show the actual product name, subscription term, price/currency and billing terms.

Current benefits

  1. removal of banner and rewarded ads
  2. photo attachments for running records
  3. two additional daily food items
  4. one additional R currency on weekly-quest completion

Daily food remains capped at three and unused food expires the next day.

Purchase and entitlement

Purchase starts from a signed-in RUNTORY account. Plus entitlement is applied only when the StoreKit transaction is verified and safely bound to the current RUNTORY user.

Renewal, cancellation and refund

The subscription may renew automatically unless cancelled in the user's Apple account under the terms shown by the App Store. Payment method, renewal, cancellation and refunds are handled through Apple's App Store payment system and applicable policies. Deleting the RUNTORY account or app does not cancel the subscription. Mandatory consumer rights are not limited by this notice.

Restore

The in-app restore function rechecks verifiable current StoreKit entitlement and applies it only after current-user binding is confirmed.
