Support

This page covers RUNTORY usage, sign-in, Friends, backup and restore, running records, advertising, subscriptions, billing, account deletion, and policy questions.

Contact

Email: support.runpet@gmail.com

Do not send passwords, Apple ID passwords, card numbers, raw receipts, or Firebase tokens. Provide only the app version, device model, approximate time, and general error text.

Account and data

  • The commercial iOS release uses Firebase Auth and Sign in with Apple.
  • Friends data is processed as account-linked data in Firebase/Firestore.
  • Supported structured data may be restored through Firestore; completed routes and original record photos may be backed up through owner-scoped Firebase Storage.
  • Signing out does not delete all local data. Use in-app account deletion to request account cleanup.

Subscription

  • RUNTORY supports monthly and yearly RUNTORY Plus purchase and restore on iOS.
  • Benefits are applied after Apple transaction processing and Firebase server verification.
  • Plus users do not receive banner or rewarded ads.
  • Deleting the account or uninstalling the app does not cancel the App Store subscription.
  • Android billing and production ads remain on hold.

Running and permissions

  • Location: active outdoor run distance and route
  • Motion & Fitness: indoor runs and step display
  • Photo library: user-selected record photos

Advertising

Free users may see Google Mobile Ads banners and rewarded ads. Google UMP checks whether consent is required before ad requests, and an advertising privacy-options entry is available in Settings where required.

The launch uses restricted non-personalized requests and does not request ATT. If consent does not permit requests or an ad fails to load, the rest of the app should remain usable.
