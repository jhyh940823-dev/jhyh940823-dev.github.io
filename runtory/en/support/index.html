Contact

For RUNTORY usage, privacy/location, account deletion, ads, Plus subscriptions or billing: support.runpet@gmail.com.

Do not send Apple ID passwords, card numbers, authentication tokens or other secrets. App version, device type, approximate occurrence time and ordinary error text are sufficient.

Current iOS account and backup

  • Authentication: Sign in with Apple
  • Remote identity/friends/account backup: Apple CloudKit
  • Durable local data: Drift/SQLite and app-managed files
  • Account backup may contain precise completed-run routes and app-managed original record-photo copies.

Subscription and ads

RUNTORY Plus is a monthly/annual auto-renewable App Store subscription verified with StoreKit 2. Restore is available in-app; cancellation is managed in the Apple account subscription settings. Deleting the RUNTORY account does not cancel the subscription.

Free users may receive Google Mobile Ads. The first iOS release uses UMP gating, Limited Ads, NPA/RDP, disables publisher first-party ID and does not request ATT.
