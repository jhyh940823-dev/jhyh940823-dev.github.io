1. Purpose

RUNTORY processes personal information needed to provide running records, pet growth, Friends, account backup and restore, iOS subscriptions, ads for free users, location-based services, and backend security.

2. Information processed

  • Firebase UID, sign-in provider data, and provider email/profile information when supplied
  • Display name, friend code, requests, relations, lists, and ranking summaries
  • Age-14+ confirmation and required-consent versions and timestamps
  • App Attest, DeviceCheck, and Firebase App Check integrity information
  • Precise location during outdoor runs, completed-run GPS route points, distance, time, pace, motion, and step data
  • Location collection/use fact records such as requester, collection/use time, collection method, acquisition path, and use method; the fact record itself does not contain latitude/longitude coordinates
  • User-selected app-managed original record photos
  • Pet, egg, challenge, inventory, badge, settings, and structured backup data
  • iOS subscription product, hashed purchase/user identifiers, verification status and time, expiration/cancellation/refund timing, and minimal transaction-correlation data
  • Advertising consent state and data that may be processed for ad delivery, such as IP-derived approximate location, app/device identifiers, advertising data, product interaction, and SDK diagnostics

RUNTORY does not directly collect or store payment-card or bank-account details.

3. Storage, processors, and cross-border processing

App data may be stored in on-device Drift/SQLite, SharedPreferences, and app documents. Firebase Auth supports sign-in; Firebase App Check supports app/device integrity; Firestore stores account/profile/Friends/structured backup data, subscription-verification ledger records, and location-use fact records; Firebase Storage stores owner-scoped completed-route and original-photo assets; Cloud Functions support subscription verification, location-use fact processing, account deletion, and retention cleanup.

Processor: RUNTORY delegates Firebase/Google Cloud to operate authentication, databases, storage, server functions, app-integrity services, and related infrastructure to the extent necessary to provide the service. Data is handled according to RUNTORY's retention/destruction rules and Google Cloud's contractual deletion procedures after the delegated task or service relationship ends.

RUNTORY's Firestore database and user-backup Firebase Storage are configured in Seoul, South Korea (asia-northeast3). Firebase Cloud Functions used for subscription verification, location-use fact processing, account deletion, and retention cleanup run in the United States (us-central1, Iowa), so information needed by those functions may be processed in the United States.

  • Processor: Google Cloud Platform, including Google Cloud Korea LLC, Google affiliates, and approved subprocessors
  • Country: United States (primary processing region us-central1, Iowa) and limited additional locations permitted under Google Cloud contractual/subprocessor terms
  • Timing/method: encrypted HTTPS/TLS when using purchase, restore, entitlement refresh, outdoor location-use fact recording, account deletion, and related server functions
  • Purpose: App Store subscription verification, safe recording of location-use facts, fraud/integrity protection, account cleanup, and retention-period management

Apple sign-in/App Store billing and information processed directly on the device by Google Mobile Ads/UMP follow the relevant provider's services and privacy terms.

4. Subscription verification

For iOS purchase and restore, StoreKit transaction and verification data is processed by Apple and RUNTORY's Firebase HTTPS verifier. Plus entitlement is applied according to server-verified status. Banner and rewarded ad requests are suppressed for RUNTORY Plus users.

5. Advertising and tracking

Google Mobile Ads banners and rewarded ads may be shown to free users. Before requesting ads, RUNTORY uses Google UMP to update consent information and show a consent form when required.

The current launch configuration uses non-personalized/restricted-data-processing signals and disables the iOS ads SDK publisher first-party identifier. RUNTORY does not request ATT and does not enable personalized or cross-app tracking. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are not currently enabled.

6. Retention, destruction procedure, and method

  • Raw GPS route geometry: up to 365 days on the device; related raw route data is removed when the user deletes the record or withdraws location consent.
  • Account/profile/Friends/backups/routes/photos: retained while needed to provide the service/account and cleaned up when the user deletes the relevant content or account.
  • Location collection/use fact records: automatically recorded as required by Korean location-information law and retained under RUNTORY policy for 6 months from the record date. If the user withdraws location consent or deletes the account, fact records without a separate legal retention basis are destroyed without delay even before six months.
  • Minimal subscription transaction/verification ledger: retained for 5 years from the last verification/processing date for legally required commerce records and settlement/dispute handling. Only minimized data such as hashed purchase/user identifiers, product/plan, verification state and time, expiration/cancellation/refund timing, and minimal correlation information is retained; payment-card and bank-account details are not retained.

When a retention period ends or the processing purpose is completed, electronic records are deleted in a manner intended to prevent practical restoration or reuse. Records that must be kept under law are segregated from normal service data, access-restricted, and destroyed after the applicable period.

The storage medium for legally retained records may change for operational reasons. For example, if RUNTORY ends the service, required records may be exported from Firebase to encrypted separate storage. The same remaining retention period, access controls, and destruction obligations continue to apply.

Deleting the RUNTORY app or account does not cancel an App Store subscription.
