Article 1 (Controller and purposes)

Meongstore processes information necessary to provide RUNTORY account authentication, running records, pet/challenge/badge gameplay, friends, account backup and restore, iOS subscriptions, advertising for free users, customer support, and compliance obligations. The current iOS account and backup path uses Sign in with Apple and Apple CloudKit. StoreKit 2 is authoritative for subscription entitlement, and Google UMP and Google Mobile Ads are used for ad consent and delivery.

Article 2 (Data processed)

  • RUNTORY user identifier, Apple sign-in identifiers, and email/display name when provided by Apple
  • required-consent versions, timestamps and locale, plus the age-14-or-older confirmation
  • public friend display name, friend code, requests/relationships and weekly running aggregates
  • precise location, completed-run GPS points/order/timestamps, distance, duration, pace, calories, motion and step data
  • photos selected by the user for running records and app-managed copies
  • pet, egg, challenge, ticket, inventory, badge, quest, settings and backup data
  • iOS subscription product, StoreKit transaction/verification state, account-binding reference and Plus entitlement state
  • ad consent and SDK information required for ad delivery. Google Mobile Ads may process IP-based approximate location, diagnostics/performance and ad-view/interaction information.

RUNTORY does not directly collect or store payment card or bank-account numbers.

Article 3 (Storage, retention and deletion)

Data may be stored in Drift/SQLite, SharedPreferences and app-managed files on the device. Local raw route geometry for a completed run is removed on the next normal app launch after the run becomes older than 365 days. Non-location summaries such as distance, duration, pace and calories may remain for record functionality.

For signed-in iOS users, a CloudKit account backup can contain structured data, precise completed-run route assets and app-managed original photo bytes. Cloud backup has a lifecycle separate from the local 365-day route cleanup. The current account-deletion path deletes the user's CloudKit backup zone.

Account identity, friends, game and settings data are retained as needed to provide the service and are deleted when the user deletes the relevant data or account, except where law requires otherwise. Location-use fact records are maintained for applicable compliance and user-rights purposes, and the current withdrawal/account-deletion flow purges records for the current user.

Article 4 (Apple services and CloudKit)

Sign in with Apple authenticates the account. CloudKit private database is used for account identity and user backup; CloudKit public database is used where the current implementation needs minimally public friend data and identity-minimized location-use fact records. A user's private database is tied to that user's iCloud account by default.

App Store and StoreKit provide subscription purchase, renewal, restore and transaction verification. Deleting a RUNTORY account or app does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription. Payment and transaction records independently held by Apple are handled under Apple's policies and legal obligations.

Article 5 (Advertising and Google services)

Free users may see Google Mobile Ads banners and rewarded ads, while Google UMP manages whether ads may be requested and applicable privacy choices. The first iOS release uses Limited Ads, non-personalized/restricted-processing requests and disables publisher first-party ID. The app does not request ATT permission and does not use NSUserTrackingUsageDescription. App-side ad requests are blocked for Plus users.

Limited Ads disables personalization and functions that require a local identifier, but it does not eliminate all network processing needed for ad delivery, security, invalid-traffic prevention or diagnostics.

Article 6 (International processing and external services)

RUNTORY uses global provider infrastructure for iOS authentication, CloudKit backup, App Store subscriptions and advertising for free users. Processing entrusted or stored abroad as necessary to provide the service is handled under the requirements of applicable law, and separate consent is obtained where the applicable law requires it. Provider subprocessors and infrastructure can change, so RUNTORY does not invent a fixed data-center country that the provider has not published.

Article 7 (Your rights)

Users can use the app's settings and record screens to delete available records/photos, withdraw location consent, log out and request account deletion. Requests to access, correct, delete or restrict personal or location information under applicable law may also be submitted to support. Reasonable identity verification may be required.

Article 8 (Permissions and safeguards)

  • Location, including background while an active run continues: outdoor run distance/route recording
  • Motion & Fitness: indoor running and step display
  • Photo library: user-selected record photos and saves
  • Sign in with Apple: iOS account authentication

RUNTORY applies user-scope separation, owner checks, sandbox/CloudKit access controls, protected network transport and release logging rules that avoid exposing precise routes.

Article 9 (Users under 14)

RUNTORY is operated for users aged 14 or older and does not provide a guardian-consent flow for users under 14. It does not separately collect date of birth or guardian information.
