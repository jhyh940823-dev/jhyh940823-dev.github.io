1. Purpose

RUNTORY processes information needed to provide running records, pet growth, Friends, account backup and restore, iOS subscriptions, ads for free users, and backend security. The commercial iOS release uses Firebase Auth, Sign in with Apple, Firebase App Check, Google UMP, and Google Mobile Ads.

2. Information processed

  • Firebase UID, sign-in provider data, and provider profile or email when supplied
  • Display name, friend code, requests, relations, list, and ranking summaries
  • Age-14+ confirmation and required-consent version and timestamp
  • App Attest, DeviceCheck, and App Check information for app/device integrity
  • Precise location, completed-run GPS route points, run distance, time, pace, motion, and step data
  • User-selected app-managed original record photos
  • Pet, egg, challenge, inventory, badge, settings, and structured backup data
  • iOS subscription product, transaction-verification data, entitlement state, and minimal transaction-correlation data
  • Advertising consent state and data that may be processed for ad delivery, including IP-derived approximate location, app/device identifiers, advertising data, product interaction, and SDK diagnostics

RUNTORY does not directly collect or store payment-card or bank-account details.

3. Storage and off-device processing

App data is stored in on-device Drift/SQLite, SharedPreferences, and app documents. Firebase Auth supports sign-in; Firebase App Check supports app/device integrity; Firestore stores account/profile/Friends/structured backup data and private entitlement; Firebase Storage stores owner-scoped completed-route and original-photo assets; Cloud Functions support subscription verification and account deletion. Apple processes sign-in, attestation, and App Store billing.

Completed-run routes and original app-managed record photos may be backed up to owner-scoped Firebase Storage. Firestore stores restoration manifests rather than public download URLs, local absolute paths, raw coordinate arrays, or image bytes. Generated overlays, result images, map snapshots, and share exports are not included in the current backup classes.

4. Subscription verification

For iOS purchase and restore, StoreKit transaction and verification data is processed by Apple and the Firebase HTTPS verifier. Durable Plus benefits are applied only after a server-verified active or grace result. Banner and rewarded ad requests are suppressed for RUNTORY Plus users.

5. Advertising and tracking

Google Mobile Ads banners and rewarded ads may be shown to free users. Before requesting ads, RUNTORY uses Google UMP to update consent information and display a consent form when required. An advertising privacy-options entry is available in Settings when required by the provider.

Launch ad requests use non-personalized and restricted-data-processing signals, and the iOS ads SDK publisher first-party identifier is disabled. RUNTORY does not request ATT and does not enable personalized or cross-app tracking for this launch configuration.

Restricted or non-personalized ads may still process IP-derived approximate location, app/device identifiers, advertising data, product interaction, and SDK diagnostics for delivery, frequency capping, reporting, security, and fraud prevention. Any future change to personalized ads, ATT, mediation, or advertising partners requires a new privacy and consent review. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are not active.

6. Retention and deletion

Raw GPS route geometry may be retained on device for up to 365 days. Users can delete records and photos, withdraw location consent, sign out, or use in-app account deletion. Account deletion requests cleanup of Firebase Auth, remote profile, Friends, structured backup, owner-scoped route/photo Storage assets, and local owner data.

App Check security artifacts, advertising consent records, and provider-level aggregate reporting/security records follow provider retention and privacy controls. RUNTORY account deletion may not remove all provider-level aggregate records. Deleting the app or RUNTORY account does not cancel an App Store subscription.

7. App permissions and capabilities

  • Location while in use/background: active outdoor runs
  • Motion & Fitness: indoor runs and step display
  • Photo library: user-selected record photos
  • Sign in with Apple: iOS authentication
  • App Attest/DeviceCheck: backend integrity without a user-facing permission prompt
  • UMP privacy options: advertising consent and choices where legally required
  • ATT: not requested in the launch binary

8. Minors

RUNTORY is intended for users aged 14 and over. Users under 14 cannot complete registration. RUNTORY does not provide a separate date-of-birth or guardian-information collection flow.
