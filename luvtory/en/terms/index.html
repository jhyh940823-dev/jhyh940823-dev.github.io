LUV and the mood market

LUV and the mood market are in-app record and interaction features, not actual stock or securities trading or investment advice. Neither the trading balance nor LUV can be bought for cash or withdrawn as cash. They are not cash income, redeemable deposits or guaranteed returns.

The trading balance is displayed as a number without a currency label; holdings are displayed as LUV. At a mood index of 50, buying 1 LUV costs 50. LUV is used in whole units: 1 for a heart attempt, 10 to create an album, 1 to add an album photo and 10 to seal a time capsule. There is no extra charge for capsule storage days. Check the cost and any applied Content Pass before confirming an action.

Under the new weekly system, each Monday starts again with a trading balance of 500 and holdings of 0 LUV. Unused balances and holdings do not carry over or receive cash refunds. The same reset applies at the first transition; missed weeks do not accumulate grants. The server uses a saved reference time zone and applies a missed reset when you reconnect. Changing the app language or travelling alone does not grant another allowance. The currency reset does not delete photos, albums or trade history.

Completing the heart is optional. Without completion, you can still use eligible content by paying its LUV cost. Completing this week's heart gives the current couple a Content Pass for the following week. The welcome pass runs from the account's signup date through the following Sunday; reinstalling, signing in again or changing partners does not extend it. An active pass automatically waives content LUV fees only, not heart attempts, subscription requirements or storage limits. An earned couple benefit does not transfer to a new partner after disconnection.

Date Draw accepts up to 3 notes per person. The weekly economy and new usage rules apply after rollout notice and transition to compatible versions. Publishing this document alone does not change balances in the current closed-test legacy app. Required advance notice and acceptance follow the terms-change rules below.

Time capsule storage limit

Each couple can keep up to 100 time capsules, including opened capsules, regardless of LUV balance. At the limit, new sealing is refused without a LUV charge. Existing capsules above the limit are not automatically deleted. Deleting a capsule you created frees a slot once fewer than 100 remain; deletion does not refund LUV. This policy does not add a daily creation allowance; existing request-rate protections remain.

Luvtory+ subscriptions

Luvtory+ is a monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription. Prices in South Korea are KRW 3,300 per month or KRW 33,000 per year; elsewhere, the store's displayed local-currency price applies. Check the actual price, period and payment conditions in the store confirmation screen before purchasing. Opening this document or accepting the terms does not make a purchase.

One valid subscription can provide benefits to both currently connected partners. Ownership and management stay with the purchaser. Shared access ends when the relationship ends. If both people already pay for subscriptions, neither is automatically cancelled or refunded.

Album creation and photo additions have LUV costs, waived only while a valid Content Pass applies. A subscription alone does not waive LUV costs. New Memory photo attachments require a subscription; written Memories and the calendar stay free. Existing Memory photos remain viewable and deletable after expiry. Deferring an aggregate storage cap is not a promise of permanent unlimited storage.

Free users can create and edit 1 album per couple. With valid Luvtory+ benefits, each couple can create up to 50 albums, including drafts, with up to 30 photos per album. Existing albums remain viewable and deletable after expiry; editing albums other than the selected free album or creating above the free limit requires a subscription. The free selection stays fixed unless that album is deleted, when another can be selected. New attachments are limited to 1 photo per Memory. Existing records above the limits are not automatically deleted. These count limits are separate from the deferred aggregate storage cap.

In-app banner ads are not shown while you have valid Luvtory+ benefits. Ad removal also applies when those benefits are shared by your currently connected partner.

Disconnection and account deletion

Either partner disconnecting ends access for both and starts deletion of all shared content of that couple, including the partner's contributions. Content cannot be restored in the app or transferred to a new relationship. Personal accounts and own subscriptions remain. Account deletion also ends the current connection and processes shared content from past relationships, without deleting the partner's personal account.

Uninstalling the app or deleting an account does not automatically cancel an Apple or Google store subscription. Use the relevant store's management and refund process, subject to applicable rights.
