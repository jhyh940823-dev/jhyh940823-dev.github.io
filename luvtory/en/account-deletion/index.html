Delete your luvtory account and data

Guidance updated: 2026-09-26 · Official launch date not yet set

You can request deletion of your luvtory account at support.runpet@gmail.com even after uninstalling the app or losing access to sign-in. The button below opens an email draft. Without an email app, copy the address into your webmail. You do not need to reinstall the app or sign in to use this page.

Send an email request

Use the subject “luvtory account deletion request”. Tell us whether you used Apple or Google to sign in, how we can reply, and that you want your account and associated data deleted. The person handling your request will explain any additional minimum information needed to locate the account.

Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or tokens, card numbers, full purchase receipts, identity-document copies or your partner’s private photos. Sending an email starts a request; it does not confirm that deletion has already finished.

Identity checks and progress

Privacy contact JungHyun Park reviews requests. A sender address or nickname alone is not proof of account ownership. We explain a safe verification step using registered sign-in information and contact methods. Tell us if your Apple Hide My Email address differs from the address you are writing from.

We distinguish receipt, identity verification, deletion in progress and completion. Your request does not delete another user’s personal account. When verification is difficult, we explain the next steps. Canceling a subscription or obtaining your partner’s approval is not a prerequisite for deleting your data.

Request deletion inside the app

When you can sign in, open Us > Us settings (gear) > My account > Account & data > Delete account. Read the shared-data impact and separate subscription notice, then reauthenticate with Google or Apple.

Signing out or uninstalling alone does not delete your server account or records. To keep your account but delete particular photos or records, use their deletion actions or specify the scope in an email request.

What is deleted and how your partner is affected

Account deletion removes your luvtory authentication account, profile, registration eligibility and terms acceptance record (if collected), and your private wallet, holdings and trading data. It ends your current connection and schedules shared photos, albums, memories, plans, anniversaries, together activities, time capsules and audio from current and past relationships for deletion.

Shared content is removed regardless of who contributed it, including content your partner added. Your partner’s personal account and own subscription remain. Deleted shared content is not transferred to a new partner and cannot be restored in the app. Copies already saved or captured outside the app cannot be recalled.

Access removal, server cleanup, retries and cleanup of interrupted uploads happen in stages. Account cleanup completion is not simultaneous physical removal of every provider backup. Once loss of access is confirmed, private app caches and widget display data are also cleared and a widget refresh is requested.

Records retained separately and periods

Minimum pseudonymous markers used to prevent receipt reuse are scheduled for cleanup based on 1 year after account cleanup completion. They are separate from financial transaction ledgers. Store-held purchase records follow the store’s policies and legal obligations.

The retention standard is 1 year after resolution for ordinary support inquiries and 1 year after case closure for private safety reports. Unresolved requests are not treated as resolved. If law or a dispute requires separate retention, the necessary records, basis and period are distinguished. We do not treat all data as indefinitely retained or promise immediate removal merely upon receiving a request.

Media storage has a 7-day soft-delete recovery period after file deletion; this does not provide an in-app restore feature. The inspected Cloud Logging buckets retain ordinary logs for 30 days and required audit logs for 400 days. Firebase states that Crashlytics begins removing data after 90 days, and residual authentication data is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days of initiating user deletion. Each period applies only to its respective records.

Sender and reply addresses, the request text and necessary attachments are handled through ordinary Gmail for intake, identity verification and replies. Google’s processing and retention information is available below. Do not include unnecessary private content.

Manage subscriptions separately

Deleting your account or app, signing out or disconnecting a couple does not automatically cancel Apple or Google subscription renewal. Check cancellation in the store where you purchased. Stopping renewal and requesting a refund of an earlier payment are separate actions. Your rights under applicable law remain.

  • Cancel an Apple subscription
  • Cancel a Google Play subscription

Your Google or Apple account is separate

Deleting luvtory does not delete your Google Account, Gmail or Apple Account. Removing luvtory’s sign-in connection at Google or Apple does not replace a request to delete luvtory data.

Inside the app, we request revocation using authorization obtained through Apple reauthentication. If an external request does not include the authorization needed for that process, we explain luvtory data deletion separately from revoking Apple access yourself. You do not need to email sign-in tokens or first complete manual Apple revocation to request data deletion.
