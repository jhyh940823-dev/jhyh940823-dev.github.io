Purchases by minors

Minors need any legally required parent or guardian consent to buy. A contract made without it may be canceled under applicable law.

The registration check for age 16 or older is not proof of adulthood or a legal guardian's consent. App language does not determine the applicable country or adulthood, and having an adult or subscribing partner is not treated as guardian consent.

Stopping renewal and requesting a refund

Unless canceled, subscriptions renew through the purchasing store on the displayed schedule and conditions. Stopping future renewals is separate from requesting a refund for amounts already paid. Check the applicable access end date and refund status in that store. Uninstalling, signing out or disconnecting from a partner does not cancel a subscription.

For Apple purchases, use Apple's refund request process; for Google Play purchases, use Google's refund process. You can also contact Luvtory support at support.runpet@gmail.com about contract cancellation, refunds or service problems. Request receipt, identity and transaction checks, the decision and the actual payment return are separate steps. A support acknowledgment is not refund approval or confirmation of an immediate payment return.

Applicable statutory withdrawal, contract-cancellation and remedies for defective or undelivered services remain available. Digital delivery does not automatically exclude every refund, and the registration-age check or terms acceptance does not waive those rights. Deadlines, exceptions and amounts depend on applicable law and store transaction terms; no single refund deadline is promised for every country.

One subscription for the current couple

If either currently connected partner has a valid subscription, both receive benefits. Purchasing, restoration, and management belong to the purchasing account. Receipts and payment ownership are not duplicated or transferred to the partner.

Shared benefits end for the non-purchaser when the relationship ends. A purchaser keeps their own valid benefits and can share with a new currently connected partner. Two existing subscriptions are not automatically cancelled or refunded.

Photos, albums, and LUV

New Memory photo attachments require Luvtory+. The calendar and written Memories stay free, and existing Memory photos remain viewable and deletable after expiry. Premium albums have additional LUV requirements.

An aggregate storage cap is currently deferred, not promised to be permanently unlimited. Any future change requires review of its scope and notices. Do not describe exceeding capacity alone as an automatic deletion policy for existing photos.

Each couple can have up to 100 albums, including drafts, with up to 30 photos per album. New photo attachments are limited to 1 photo per Memory. Existing records above these limits are not automatically deleted. These count limits are separate from the deferred aggregate storage cap.

Price and renewal

The planned subscription prices for South Korea are KRW 3,300 monthly and KRW 33,000 annually. The annual price is KRW 6,600 less than twelve monthly payments. Overseas prices use each store's automatic currency conversion. Check the price and billing period shown by the store before purchasing. A sales start date, free trial and additional promotions have not been set.

To stop automatic renewal, use the purchasing store's subscription management. Period changes, renewal, refunds, and expiry are reflected from verified store status.

Restore and cancel

Restore an existing purchase using its purchasing store account and associated Luvtory account. Purchase success and benefits are not considered complete before server verification. Contact support with the device, time, and general error rather than a complete receipt or card details.

Deleting the app or account does not automatically cancel a subscription.
