Information and purposes

  • Authentication: Firebase user identifiers, Apple or Google sign-in information, email and display name when provided, and profiles you create.
  • Couple connections: invitations, connection and disconnection records, member identifiers, and relationship-based access permissions.
  • Records and sharing: moods, in-app trades, plans, anniversaries, Memories, selected profile and album photos, Together records, time-capsule messages, and voice recordings.
  • Subscriptions: store transactions and verification information, account-binding references, expiry and refund status, and current couple access. Luvtory does not directly store payment card numbers.
  • Security and diagnostics: App Check attestations, server request and error information, and Crashlytics error and device diagnostics.
  • Support: contact details and messages you send by email. Do not send sensitive material that is unnecessary to investigate your request.

Sources of information and your choices

Information comes from records, photos and audio you enter or select; records shared by your connected partner; and authentication or transaction information provided by Google, Apple and the purchasing store. Authentication and security request data is processed when you use the app and servers. Optional diagnostics and advertising SDK processing are described separately.

Protected account features require authentication and security checks. Photo or audio attachments and diagnostic sharing are optional; declining them does not disable all text records. This policy explains processing: accepting the Terms of Use is not blanket consent to advertising, tracking, optional diagnostics or other processing that requires separate consent.

Sharing with your partner

Records are shared with your current partner according to each feature's permissions; personal accounts and payment receipts are not transferred to them. Under the approved new policy, either partner disconnecting ends shared access for both and queues that couple's shared content for deletion. Previous content is not transferred to a new partner. Personal wallets, holdings and individual buy/sell records are visible only to their owner. However, weekly records shared with both partners may include each person’s trade counts and realized profit/loss summaries displayed by the feature. Private individual trades are distinct from these shared summaries.

The app cannot retrieve copies your partner has already viewed, downloaded, or captured outside it. Obtain the necessary rights and consent before uploading another person's photos or voice.

Security measures

Connections to Firebase use HTTPS. Server and storage access rules check account ownership and current relationship permissions. App Check verifies the app or device, and important change requests have input validation, rate limits and duplicate-operation protection. Reports and personal billing information are separate from records readable by a partner.

These measures do not guarantee that every security incident can be prevented. Photo previews and sharing authorization are handled on servers; this is not an end-to-end encrypted service where only user devices can decrypt the contents. Do not send unnecessary photos or authentication information in support requests. This explanation does not exclude statutory security duties or the operator's legal responsibility.

Processing grounds and choices

Authentication, storing and sharing records, requested features and account management support the provision of the service to users. Processing for legal obligations or abuse prevention is limited to those purposes. Optional error diagnostics use a separate choice; terms acceptance is not bundled consent to advertising, diagnostics or international transfers.

For overseas outsourcing or storage necessary to provide the contracted service, our Korean disclosure assessment uses Article 28-8(1)(3) of the Personal Information Protection Act. This does not automatically cover all optional diagnostics or advertising. Processing that requires separate consent also requires the relevant notice and consent. Merely reading this policy or sending email is not treated as blanket consent.

External processing of accounts, records and media

Google Cloud/Firebase supplies Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions and App Check. The Korean Cloud contracting entity is Google Cloud Korea LLC; Google LLC and service-specific subprocessors participate in operation and maintenance. Privacy inquiries can use the Firebase contact below or Luvtory support.

Authentication identifiers, email and authentication-request data are processed by Firebase Authentication in the United States. The Firestore database for accounts, relationships, moods, calendar and transaction records is in the United States (nam5). Selected photos and audio use the couple's designated storage path in the United States (us-central1), Belgium (europe-west1) or South Korea (asia-northeast3).

The media route is chosen when the couple is created using the inviting device's time zone: the Americas use the United States; Europe, Africa and Atlantic zones use Belgium; Asia, Australia, Pacific and Indian zones use South Korea; other zones use the United States. Travel or language changes do not automatically move existing files.

Encrypted network transfers occur during sign-in and token refresh, record reads and changes, uploads, downloads, background synchronization and server processing. Purposes are authentication, storage and sharing, previews, authorization and service protection. Retention follows the data-specific periods below.

A selected storage country is not the complete set of support or maintenance access locations. Google uses global infrastructure and limited-authority subprocessors according to the service. Its official lists distinguish entities, countries, activities, services and regions. Sharing material for an external Cloud support request is separate from routine storage; the entire list is not a list of recipients of every photo.

Users who do not want overseas authentication or storage may stop account use and request deletion in the app or by email. There is no domestic-only account/storage option; refusing processing necessary for those account features prevents their provision.

App verification and optional diagnostics

App Check is required security processing to verify app requests. Android uses Google Play Integrity; iOS uses Apple App Attest with DeviceCheck support. App/device attestations and tokens protect requests. Firebase does not retain attestation material; replay-protection tokens may be retained for up to 30 days. Google and Apple also process attestation under their own policies.

Firebase Crashlytics transmission is off by default on a new installation and can be chosen in our settings under error-diagnostic sharing. Reports may include error categories and call locations, installation identifiers and app/device diagnostics to investigate faults and improve stability. The app restricts its own reporting of record contents, photo URLs and authentication tokens; that does not make every SDK field anonymous.

For Korean businesses the Crashlytics contracting entity is Google Asia Pacific Pte. Ltd., with service providers including Firebase, Inc. Firebase describes global processing for Crashlytics and App Check rather than a single-country guarantee. The service-specific official locations and subprocessor lists below describe that scope. A Singapore contracting entity does not mean Singapore-only storage.

Turn sharing off and restart the app to stop subsequent transmission. The app requests deletion of unsent reports before a new opt-in. Local SDK storage and reports already sent are separate: Firebase retains Crashlytics reports for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems. This preference does not control the advertising SDK.

Retention and deletion

  • Accounts, profiles and registration-age confirmation are used while the account is maintained and removed through account deletion. Dates of birth and identity documents are not collected for the age check.
  • Shared photos, albums, records and capsules are deleted when the user deletes them or either partner disconnects, regardless of authorship. They are not carried into a new relationship. Account deletion includes personal data and shared material from current and past relationships.
  • Access termination and deletion scheduling are followed by staged cleanup and retries, including in-flight upload remnants. This is not instant physical removal of every copy. Deleted Storage objects have a 7-day recovery retention and are not accessible through normal app reads.
  • Cloud Logging retains ordinary logs for 30 days and required audit logs for 400 days. After authentication deletion starts, Firebase removes residual authentication data from live and backup systems within 180 days under its policy.
  • Reports contain reasons, descriptions, targets, minimal identifiers and processing times privately, without automatic copying of original photos. They are retained for 1 year after resolution and periodically deleted. Account deletion does not automatically resolve an open report.
  • Minimal pseudonymous receipt-reuse markers have a retention standard of 1 year after completed account deletion. They are not statutory transaction ledgers; legacy billing records require separate migration/cleanup. New purchases are currently blocked.
  • Records subject to a legal preservation duty are separated for that purpose. Where Korean e-commerce law applies, contract/withdrawal and payment/supply records are retained for 5 years, consumer complaints/disputes for 3 years and display/advertising records for 6 months. These periods are not applied indiscriminately to every support email.
  • Data is deleted after its purpose or period ends, with electronic records removed in a manner intended to prevent recovery. Provider backup/security remnants and legal preservation exceptions are distinct from continued normal service use. The app cannot retrieve copies a partner saved outside it.

Retention of blocking records

When you explicitly block a partner, we separately retain the blocking and blocked account identifiers, relationship identifier, the partner nickname available at the time, and the blocking time. These records prevent reconnection and display your block list. Email addresses, profile photos and report descriptions are not copied into blocking records. The partner and third parties cannot read this list.

An ordinary disconnection does not create a block. Blocking records remain after disconnection and shared-content deletion until you unblock the partner or the account-deletion process removes records related to either account. Accepting a deletion request and completing cleanup may occur at different times. This is separate from the one-year retention after a reported case is closed.

Device storage and acceptance records

Photo caches, sign-in state and widget display copies of photos, moods and nicknames may be stored on the device. Once sign-out, account switching or the end of couple access is confirmed, the app requests cleanup and widget refresh. OS refresh timing and copies captured outside the app are not fully controllable.

Android excludes app data from cloud backup and device transfer. iOS does not use the same blanket exclusion, so some preferences may return with a backup restore. Check diagnostic choices in our settings.

Terms acceptance is currently disabled. Once enabled, the account's document version, address, server acceptance time and verification marker are kept as one current record, replaced on acceptance of a new version and removed through deletion. Past use is not retrospectively recorded as consent.

Exercising rights and their scope

Where applicable law provides these rights, you may request a copy of your personal information, portability, object to processing or withdraw consent, in addition to access, correction, deletion or restriction. Requests through a legal or authorized representative require only necessary verification of their authority. The absence of an in-app export button is not a reason to refuse a statutory request. We also protect your partner's rights and private information. If records include another person's information, we consider redaction or separation to identify what can be provided, rather than refusing the entire request for that reason.

Send requests to the contact above. We respond within the applicable statutory deadline and explain any lawful restriction or necessary extension and the relevant procedures. Withdrawal of consent does not invalidate lawful processing before withdrawal. Choices about diagnostics and advertising, account deletion and legally required retention are separate; not every request results in immediate, unconditional deletion.

For access requests under Korean privacy law, access is generally provided within 10 days of receipt. Where a statutory reason requires restriction, deferral or refusal, we explain the reason and objection procedure within that period; deferred access is provided without delay once the reason ends. For rights requests subject to the GDPR, we generally respond within one month. An extension of up to two further months for complexity or number of requests requires an explanation within the first month and is not automatic. These deadlines are not applied indiscriminately to other countries or every request type.
