Stopping renewal and requesting a refund

Unless canceled, subscriptions renew through the purchasing store on the displayed schedule and conditions. Stopping future renewals is separate from requesting a refund for amounts already paid. Check the applicable access end date and refund status in that store. Uninstalling, signing out or disconnecting from a partner does not cancel a subscription.

For Apple purchases, use Apple's refund request process; for Google Play purchases, use Google's refund process. You can also contact Luvtory support at support.runpet@gmail.com about contract cancellation, refunds or service problems. Request receipt, identity and transaction checks, the decision and the actual payment return are separate steps. A support acknowledgment is not refund approval or confirmation of an immediate payment return.

Applicable statutory withdrawal, contract-cancellation and remedies for defective or undelivered services remain available. Digital delivery does not automatically exclude every refund, and the registration-age check or terms acceptance does not waive those rights. Deadlines, exceptions and amounts depend on applicable law and store transaction terms; no single refund deadline is promised for every country.

What to include

  • The app name (Luvtory), app version, device model, and operating-system version.
  • When the issue occurred, the action you were taking, and the general error message.
  • Only if needed, a screenshot with personal information and your partner's photos concealed.

What not to send

Do not send passwords, verification codes, tokens, card numbers, complete receipts, or identity-document copies. Do not include more of your partner's private content than is strictly necessary for the issue.

Accounts and subscriptions

In the app, open Us > Us settings (gear) > Account and data > Delete account. If you cannot use the app, email support.runpet@gmail.com with the subject 'Luvtory account deletion request', your sign-in method and your intention to delete the account. We will explain a secure identity check. An email address or nickname alone is not sufficient proof of account ownership.

Purchases, restoration, and cancellation are tied to the purchasing Apple or Google account. Receipt ownership is not arbitrarily transferred to another Luvtory account. Deleting an account does not automatically cancel a store subscription.

Safety and privacy concerns

You can also report privacy violations, harassment, unwanted connections, or content concerns through this contact. Disconnect an unwanted relationship where possible. For immediate physical danger, seek assistance from local emergency services.

We will explain any necessary checks and the outcome of your request. Receipt of a request does not mean that enforcement, deletion or a refund has been completed.

Ordinary support inquiries are retained for 1 year after the inquiry is resolved. Under the operating plan, privacy contact 박정현 reviews and deletes expired inquiries on the first business day of each month. Records required for legal obligations or disputes are handled separately with their scope, grounds and period identified. Support uses ordinary Gmail to process your address, message and necessary attachments for handling and answering your inquiry. Deletion from the mailbox and removal of the provider's residual copies may occur at different times.
